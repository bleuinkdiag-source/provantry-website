Provantry

Know what's in your pantry, before you shop.

Scan groceries as you put them away, and Provantry keeps track of what you have, where it is, and when it expires — so you stop buying duplicates and stop throwing food out.

  • Scan

    Add items in seconds

    Scan a barcode or type it in by hand. Provantry looks up the product automatically — you just confirm the quantity and where it's going.

  • Track

    Never lose track of expiration dates

    Set a date when you scan an item, and your whole inventory stays organized — nothing forgotten in the back of the fridge.

  • Organize

    Locations that match your kitchen

    Fridge, Freezer, Pantry, or anything else — set up storage locations the way your household actually works.

  • Plan

    Meal planning, when you're ready

    Provantry Pro adds a meal calendar and reusable recipe templates that check your inventory before you cook.
